# Roadmap

## Planned

**A `tphone` hook.**
[terminalphone](https://github.com/jamesponwith/terminalphone)
is the Rust rewrite of the party line. Blocked on its group
relay (still pending upstream). Note: it embeds Arti.

## Tabled

**Nostr.** No existing client met the bar for subscriber UX.
May revisit if the ecosystem matures.
## Not planned

**Occupancy tracking.** Metadata trail about who was where.

**Catch-up messages.** "You missed a room" is a record of
absence.

**WhatsApp.** See
[Chat Networks](configuration/adapters.md#two-caveats).

**Meta/any Facebook service.** No.
